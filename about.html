Professional Experience

Since 2022, I’ve worked in clinical and translational research at Columbia University Medical Center. I began as a lab technician in the Environmental Health Sciences Department, leading academic studies on neurodegenerative diseases like ALS and Parkinson’s disease. During this time, I troubleshooted our lab’s cellular protocols, took care of our mouse cohorts, and conducted dissections and tissue sectioning days.

In 2024, I moved to the Pediatrics Pulmonary division as the lead clinical research coordinator, where I managed the day-to-day work of five Cystic Fibrosis clinical trials. I handled ethics board submissions, kept regulatory records in order, worked directly with patients and samples, and collaborated with a pulmonary team in Cuenca, Ecuador on a quality improvement project.

Of note, as an undergraduate at Wesleyan University, I tutored fellow students in introductory biology and lab courses and volunteered as a tutor and mentor for a foster youth program. Now at Mailman, I am the first-year representative for the Global Learning Exchange Club, as I aim to futher my gloabal health education during my studies.
